About Us

What we pour

Syris Cafe is a small, pink-lit corner for slow mornings and sweet afternoons. We make every drink by hand, from a bold espresso to a rose latte, and we bake our matcha mousse cake and cinnamon rolls in-house.

How it began

It started with a love of matcha, a stubborn coffee machine and a few friends who kept asking for one more cup. That little habit grew into Syris, a place where the drinks are made with care and everyone gets a comfy seat.

Sip softly, stay a while.
